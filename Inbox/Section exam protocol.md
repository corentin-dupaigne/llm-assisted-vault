# Section exam protocol

Every Saturday, Claude runs the exam as a real interviewer, in English. The goal is to measure, not to learn: no notes open during the exam.

## Algo part

- **2 problems I have never seen**, on the pattern of the week's deep dive, picked by Claude
- **25 minutes each**, out loud, applying [[Coding Interview Method]]
- **Pass** if both are true:
  - both solutions are correct, and I give their time and space complexity
  - I used **at most 1 hint** across the two problems

## SRE part (weeks that have one)

- **5 oral questions**, interview style, like the Nov 25 diagnostic
- **"Explain [topic] to me in 3 minutes"**
- **Pass** at 4 out of 5, with the 3-minute explanation in the right order and without major gaps

## Recall question

- **1 question from an earlier section**, without saying which one. It's not graded, it's a recall check: if I miss it, that topic goes back into NeetTrack and Anki reviews.

## After the exam

- **Pass** → next section.
- **Fail** → I still move on, but the failed topic takes next week's deep dive slot, and I retake the exam the following Saturday.
- Write down in the topic's notes ("My mistakes") what made me fail.
